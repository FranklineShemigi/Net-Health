THEME:
An AI-powered medication safety platform that helps healthcare students practice clinical decisions and helps licensed professionals verify medication safety.

MISSION:
To reduce medication errors by giving healthcare students and professionals a safe place to learn, practice, and verify medication administration.

GOAL:
Prepares future healthcare professionals to make safer medication decisions.

NORTH STAR
Safer medication decisions through practice, knowledge, and technology

DISCLAIMER
DoseWise Pro is an educational platform and does not replace professional clinical judgment or official prescribing guidelines.
