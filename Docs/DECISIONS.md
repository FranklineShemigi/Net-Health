# Net-Health Decisions Log

---

## Decision 001

**Date:** 2026-07-19

**Decision**
Net-Health will focus on clinical learning, medication safety, and telehealth.

**Reason**
Building a Hospital Information System (HIS) or Electronic Medical Record (EMR) would greatly increase the project's complexity and shift it away from its primary mission.

**Impact**
The platform remains focused on education, simulation, and patient consultations with verified healthcare professionals.

---

## Decision 002

**Date:** 2026-07-19

**Decision**
Every user selects a role during registration.

**Roles**
- Student
- Doctor
- Nurse
- Pharmacist
- Clinical Officer
- Patient
- Educator
- Administrator

**Reason**
Different users require different tools and dashboards.

---

## Decision 003

**Date:** 2026-07-19

**Decision**
AI will generate clinical scenarios but will rely on curated medical knowledge and evidence-based guidance. It will not invent unsafe medical advice.

**Reason**
Patient safety and educational accuracy are core principles of Net-Health.
