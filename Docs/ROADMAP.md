# Net-Health Roadmap

## Project Vision

Net-Health is an AI-powered clinical learning and medication safety platform designed to help healthcare students and professionals improve medication knowledge, calculate safe doses, identify drug interactions, practise clinical decision-making, and reduce medication errors.

# Long-Term Vision

Net-Health aims to become a comprehensive clinical education and medication safety platform for:

- Medical students
- Nursing students
- Pharmacy students
- Clinical officers
- Interns
- Healthcare professionals

The platform focuses on improving patient safety through education, clinical decision support, and modern AI technology.
